**Outputs:**

1. **Glucose** (C6H12O6): Plants produce glucose as a primary product of photosynthesis, which can be used for energy or building blocks.
2. **Oxygen** (O2): Oxygen atoms released during water splitting are released into the atmosphere as a byproduct of photosynthesis.
