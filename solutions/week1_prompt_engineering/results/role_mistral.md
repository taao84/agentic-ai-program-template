Water (H2O) and carbon dioxide (CO2) are used, while oxygen (O2) is released as a byproduct. 3. Ultimately, plants convert the glucose into various carbohydrates like starch or sugars, which they store for their own use or release as fruit, flowers, or seeds for other organisms to consume.
